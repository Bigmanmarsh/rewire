Search that rewires its own graph every time it misses.

Vector search finds the paragraph that sounds like your question. It can't find the second paragraph you need, the one the first paragraph points to. Rewire follows those links in HydraDB. When a link is missing, it works out why and fixes that kind of link across the whole graph. The score below is measured on questions it has never learned from.

    How it works

    1. Corpus: Wikipedia paragraphs from HotpotQA, pooled into one shared index. Every question needs two of them.
    2. Graph in HydraDB: each paragraph is a :Doc node, with an edge -[:MENTIONS {rule}]-> wherever its text names another paragraph's title.
    3. Retrieval: MiniLM picks the seed paragraphs. HydraDB then traverses their in- and out-links, and linked paragraphs are boosted by how strong their seed is.
    4. Learning: on each training miss it checks which naming rule would have created the missing link. "Surname only", "drop (qualifier)" and "acronym" are examples. It keeps a rule only if it fixes more past questions than it breaks, then re-tunes the traversal weights.
    5. Honest scoring: The held-out questions are never used for learning.
